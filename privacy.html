PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Short version: Kobö has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The game shows optional video ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect device and ad-related data to show and measure those ads, as described below. Purchases and Game Center are handled by Apple.

WHO WE ARE

Kobö ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile game made by Leonardo Pereda. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at koboadse@gmail.com.

INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

To save your game, Kobö stores the following on your device only: coins, scores, levels cleared, power-ups, characters and themes you have unlocked, quest and badge progress, daily check-in dates, your settings, and whether you own the Starter Pack or Kobö Plus. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app removes it, and Settings → Reset in the game clears your progress.

ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

Kobö offers optional rewarded video ads, which play only when you choose them (to continue a run or to earn free coins). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, viewed, clicked or completed) and diagnostic data

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and improve ads, to prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, to show personalised ads. If you do not allow tracking, ads are non-personalised. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, through Google's consent message.

IN-APP PURCHASES

Kobö offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a Starter Pack and Kobö Plus) and Apple offer codes. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, payment details or billing address. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

GAME CENTER

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Kobö sends your scores and achievements to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings, and you can turn it off in iPhone Settings → Game Center. We do not receive your Game Center identity.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you allow notifications, Kobö schedules reminders (for example, that your daily reward is ready). These are created on your device; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Kobö.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

  • We do not ask for or store your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers or analytics that collect your data.

CHILDREN

Kobö is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

  • Tracking: allow or deny it in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad personalisation: where shown, use the consent message in the app to change your choice.
  • Game data: reset it in the game's Settings, or delete the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK or California), you may have rights to access or delete data. For data collected by Google for ads, you can use Google My Ad Center. For anything else, email us.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
