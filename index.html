A fast, colorful arcade climber. Steer up the screen and pass only through the platforms that match your color. Hit the wrong one and the run is over!

COMING SOON TO THE APP STORE

Free to play on iPhone and iPad.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Slide your finger left and right to steer. Your character climbs on its own.
  2. Match your color. Platforms in your color are safe. Every other color ends the run.
  3. Read the words. From level 8, some platforms show a color name. On those, the word counts, not the platform's color.
  4. Grab coins and spend them on power-ups and new characters.

WHAT'S INSIDE

10 LEVELS + ENDLESS

Each level adds something new: more colors, shape cut-outs, speed boosts and color words. Then see how high you can climb in Endless mode.

15 CHARACTERS

Pup, Kitty, Froggy, Penguin, Panda, Robo, Ninja, Dragon and more, each with its own signature trail.

QUEST BOARD

Play over time to earn exclusive characters and menu themes that can't be bought anywhere.

POWER-UPS

Invincible, Magnet, Extra Life and Shooter. Plus daily check-in rewards and free coins every day.

GAME CENTER

Two leaderboards and 19 achievements to chase with your friends.

RETRO SOUND

Original 8-bit music and sound effects, made for the game.

ABOUT

Kobö is an independent game designed and built by Leonardo Pereda. No big studio, just one person who loves arcade games.

Kobö is free to play. It has optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a one-time Starter Pack and Kobö Plus) and optional video ads that you choose to watch, to continue a run or earn free coins. There are no forced ads.

SUPPORT THE GAME

Enjoying Kobö? A small tip helps pay for new levels, characters and music. It's completely optional and doesn't unlock anything in the game.
