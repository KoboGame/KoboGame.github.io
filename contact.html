COMMON QUESTIONS

I bought Kobö Plus but it's gone after reinstalling.
Open the game and go to Settings → RESTORE. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
I bought coins but didn't get them.
Close the game completely and open it again; pending purchases are delivered at launch. If they still don't arrive, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Do coin packs come back when I reinstall?
No. Coins and progress are saved on your device only, so deleting the game removes them. Kobö Plus can always be restored.
How do I redeem a code?
Go to Settings → REDEEM A CODE in the game, or redeem it in the App Store app under your account.
Do I have to watch ads?
No. Video ads only play when you choose them, to continue a run or to get free coins.
How do I turn off reminders?
iPhone Settings → Notifications → Kobö.
How do I start over?
Settings → RESET in the game. It clears your progress and coins; Kobö Plus is kept.
